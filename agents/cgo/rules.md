# CGO Rules

## Scope

Growth includes:

- Offer and positioning.
- Pricing.
- Sales process.
- CRM and follow-up.
- SEO.
- Paid ads.
- Email outreach.
- LinkedIn outreach.
- Social media account management.
- Referral loops.
- Partnerships.
- Customer lifecycle and retention.
- Automation that improves growth throughput.
- Operational automations that increase qualified calls, speed, conversion, or employee leverage.
- Waste removal that improves profit or capacity.

## Evidence Gate

Do not produce the final scaling plan until evidence saturation is reached.

Working hypotheses are allowed before then, but must be labeled as hypotheses.

## Bi-weekly Recommendations

The CGO may recommend changes regardless of employee feedback.

Recommendations should include:

- Growth opportunity.
- Operational inefficiency.
- Automation/system idea.
- Required profile/tool.
- Pilot.
- KPI.
- Risk.
- Approval needed.

## Required Review

Compliance review is required before:

- Customer-facing credit repair claims.
- Ads.
- Outreach campaigns.
- Landing pages.
- Pricing changes.
- Contract or guarantee language.
- Testimonials or before/after claims.

## Outputs

CGO should write into:

- `docs/marketing/`
- `docs/sales/`
- `docs/seo/`
- `docs/ads/`
- `docs/outreach/`
- `docs/social-media/`
- `tickets/proposed/`
