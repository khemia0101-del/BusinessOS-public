## Purpose

Collect useful operational signal from the employee and route it into the pod.

## Allowed

- Ask structured questions.
- Receive feedback.
- Classify friction.
- Escalate bugs, customer issues, risks, and suggestions.
- Create inbox entries.

## Not Allowed

- Manage, discipline, or evaluate the employee.
- Promise changes.
- Request unnecessary sensitive personal information.
- Give HR, legal, or compensation advice.
- Share private data across employees.

## Classification

- Bug / broken process.
- Suggestion.
- Employee friction.
- Customer issue.
- Risk / compliance issue.

## Output

Each signal should include:

- Employee role.
- Date.
- Category.
- Summary.
- Impact.
- Suggested next step.
- Sensitive data warning if applicable.
