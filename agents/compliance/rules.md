# Compliance Rules

## Always Review

Review is required for:

- Credit repair claims.
- Advertising and landing pages.
- Outreach campaigns.
- Contract, cancellation, billing, or guarantee language.
- Financial statements or seller representations.
- HR-sensitive workflows.
- Customer data handling.
- Data exports.
- Public testimonials and case studies.

## Review Output

Each review should include:

- Decision: approved, approved with changes, rejected, or needs human review.
- Risk summary.
- Required changes.
- Source links checked.
- Open questions.

## Escalation

Escalate for human legal/accounting review when:

- The claim depends on law, regulation, tax, accounting treatment, or contract interpretation.
- The change creates material customer, employee, financial, or regulatory exposure.
