# Document Everything Policy

## Rule

Every meaningful business signal must become durable context.

## Why

Documentation enables Hermes to:

- Spot anomalies.
- Detect inefficiencies.
- Find repeated customer/employee problems.
- Catch revenue leaks.
- Create proactive suggestions.
- Improve follow-up.
- Understand what was promised and by whom.
- Maintain compliance context.
- Evaluate decisions over time.

## Channels

Document relevant signal from:

- Email.
- Phone calls.
- Meetings.
- CRM.
- Calendar.
- Telegram.
- Sendblue/iMessage/SMS.
- Slack or chat.
- Social media.
- Reddit/community.
- Ads platforms.
- Website forms.
- Support systems.
- Referral partner conversations.

## Privacy And Data Minimization

Document business-relevant summaries. Do not duplicate sensitive raw personal data unless necessary and approved.

Sensitive source documents should remain in source systems. Local memory entries should link to sources where possible.
