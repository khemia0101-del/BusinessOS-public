# Measurement Standard

## Purpose

Every growth or automation initiative needs a measurement plan.

## Required Metrics

Each initiative should define:

- Primary KPI.
- Secondary KPIs.
- Baseline.
- Expected movement.
- Measurement source.
- Review cadence.
- Stop condition.
- Success threshold.

## Common KPIs

- Revenue.
- Gross profit.
- Leads.
- Qualified leads.
- Booked calls.
- Show rate.
- Close rate.
- CAC.
- LTV.
- Referral partner activation.
- Reply rate.
- Positive reply rate.
- Conversion rate.
- Time to first response.
- Manual hours saved.
- Error rate.
- Customer complaints.
- Compliance incidents.

## Reporting

Weekly reports track active execution and early KPI movement.

Bi-weekly Strategic Change Reviews decide whether to scale, stop, or change direction.

Monthly reports evaluate business-level impact.
