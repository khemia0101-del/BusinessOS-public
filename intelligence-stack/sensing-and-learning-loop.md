# Sensing And Learning Loop

## Purpose

Make Hermes continuously observe the business, interpret signal, act through controlled initiatives, and learn from results.

## Sensing

Hermes should watch:

- Leads.
- CRM stages.
- Website forms.
- Response time.
- Booked calls.
- No-shows.
- Close rate.
- Referral partner activity.
- Emails/replies.
- Reviews.
- SEO rankings.
- Ads metrics.
- Social engagement.
- Customer complaints.
- Employee friction.
- Support tickets.
- Costs and subscriptions.
- Workflow errors.

## Interpretation

Signals become:

- Anomalies.
- Recurring patterns.
- Growth opportunities.
- Inefficiency findings.
- Compliance risks.
- Missing tools/permissions.
- Candidate automation initiatives.

## Learning

After every initiative:

- Record what changed.
- Record results.
- Compare against baseline.
- Update the relevant playbook.
- Update stop conditions and eval criteria.
- Create new capabilities if the workflow recurs.
- Retire bad ideas.
