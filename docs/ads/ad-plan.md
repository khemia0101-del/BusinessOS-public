# Ad Plan

## Purpose

Plan paid acquisition tests with compliance review and measurable KPIs.

## Required Inputs

- Current ad accounts.
- Historical spend.
- CAC.
- Lead quality.
- Conversion rate.
- Approved landing pages.
- Compliance-approved claims.

## Candidate Channels

- Google Search.
- Meta.
- YouTube.
- Retargeting.

## Guardrails

- No ads launch without approved ticket.
- No claims, guarantees, timelines, or testimonials without Compliance review.
- Every campaign needs a budget cap, KPI, measurement plan, and stop condition.
