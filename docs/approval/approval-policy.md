# Approval Policy

## Required Approvers

Owner approval and partner approval are required for material changes.

## Material Changes

- Pricing.
- Offer.
- Target customer.
- Sales process.
- Service line.
- Hiring, firing, or role restructuring.
- Core workflow.
- Core software.
- Acquisition channel strategy.
- Customer-facing claims.
- Ads, outreach, and automated communications.
- Financial, legal, HR, tax, or compliance-sensitive decisions.

## Approval Record

Each approval should be recorded in the relevant ticket and `audit/decisions.md`.
