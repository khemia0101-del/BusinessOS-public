# Underwriting Evidence Checklist

Use this beside `underwriting-standard.md`. A report may be decision-ready with documented exceptions; it is not final while an undisclosed material gap remains.

## Transaction and identity

- [ ] Legal entities, ownership, proposed structure, price, financing, and closing conditions identified.
- [ ] Material contracts, related parties, change-of-control provisions, and owner dependencies mapped.

## Financial reconstruction

- [ ] Three full fiscal years and trailing period mapped when available.
- [ ] Profit and loss reconciled to tax returns.
- [ ] Revenue reconciled to bank, processor, invoice, job, order, or CRM evidence.
- [ ] Balance sheet accounts, debt, liens, leases, taxes, and off-balance-sheet obligations tested.
- [ ] Payroll and contractors reconciled to the people and role baseline.
- [ ] Seller add-backs tested for recurrence, support, and buyer replacement cost.
- [ ] Working capital, cash conversion, maintenance capital expenditure, and minimum cash estimated.

## Revenue and operations

- [ ] Customer, product or service, channel, geography, and period concentration analyzed where data permits.
- [ ] Recurring, repeat, backlog, cancellation, refund, discount, and bad-debt behavior tested where relevant.
- [ ] Lead-to-cash and delivery workflows mapped with ownership, timing, exceptions, and system use.
- [ ] Capacity, cycle time, rework, service quality, and key bottlenecks established.
- [ ] Missing capabilities and data-quality problems identified from evidence.

## People, systems, and risk

- [ ] Organization, compensation, tenure, responsibilities, owner dependence, and key-person risk mapped.
- [ ] Core software, access, data ownership, security, integrations, and continuity mapped.
- [ ] Legal, regulatory, tax, employment, privacy, insurance, licensing, and reputation risks reviewed.
- [ ] Market conditions, competition, and customer alternatives researched with current sources.

## Decision quality

- [ ] Facts, calculations, seller claims, external research, and assumptions are separated.
- [ ] Material claims cite stable evidence IDs.
- [ ] Conflicting sources and unresolved differences are visible.
- [ ] Base, downside, and severe-downside cases are modeled.
- [ ] Risks translate into a mitigation, deal protection, condition, price adjustment, or walk-away rule.
- [ ] Every low-confidence conclusion that can change price or terms appears in the executive brief.
- [ ] The machine-readable report validates against `schemas/underwriting.schema.json`.
