# Email Outreach

## Purpose

Design and track email outreach only after target, offer, compliance, deliverability, and data-source rules are clear.

## Required Inputs

- Target segment.
- Lead source.
- Suppression list.
- Sending domain.
- Email platform.
- Compliance-approved copy.
- Unsubscribe handling.

## Guardrails

- No campaign launches without approved ticket.
- Do not send to purchased or scraped lists unless explicitly approved and legally reviewed.
- Include opt-out mechanics.
- Track reply rate, positive reply rate, booked calls, conversion, complaints, and unsubscribes.
