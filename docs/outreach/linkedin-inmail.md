# LinkedIn InMail

## Purpose

Plan LinkedIn outreach as a controlled, measured channel.

## Required Inputs

- Target persona.
- LinkedIn account or Sales Navigator access.
- Message copy.
- Daily volume cap.
- Compliance review.
- CRM logging workflow.

## Guardrails

- No automated spam.
- No unsupported claims.
- No promises of outcomes.
- Track acceptance rate, reply rate, positive reply rate, booked calls, and blocked/reported signals.
