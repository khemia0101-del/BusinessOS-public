# Business Pod Context

Read `HERMES_START.md` first.

This folder is the agent operating layer. The Google Drive URL in `.env` is the data room.

Read-only work is automatically approved. Map, index, summarize, cross-reference, and document business evidence without waiting for approval.

Use workstreams for autonomous read-only/internal work. Use tickets only for approval gates, external writes, missing permissions, deployments, spend, delegated execution, and rollback-tracked changes.

Create read-only/planning Hermes profiles from `hermes-profiles/` when needed. Request approval before giving any profile write, deploy, spend, customer messaging, HR, legal, compliance, or finance-sensitive authority.

Final underwriting and final scaling plans require evidence saturation.

Continuously use:

- `growth-system/` for proactive and owner-directed growth/automation initiatives.
- `operations-system/` for inefficiency, waste, lead-flow, and workflow automation detection.
- `memory-system/` to document business communications and signals.
- `quality-system/` for zero-trust evals, tests, simulations, ship decisions, and rollback standards.
- `intelligence-stack/` for Edge Digital Twins, organizational drag scoring, sensing, and learning loops.
