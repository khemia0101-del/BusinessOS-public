# Change Risk Levels

## Level 0: Internal Read-Only

Examples: data-room indexing, internal summaries, local analysis.

Approval: automatic.

Eval: lightweight self-check.

## Level 1: Internal Build

Examples: local utility, draft workflow, report generator with test data.

Approval: automatic unless sensitive data is involved.

Eval: reviewer or test before relying on output.

## Level 2: External Draft / Non-Live

Examples: WordPress draft, email draft, campaign plan, CRM automation in dry run.

Approval: may be automatic for draft creation; approval required for live action.

Eval: independent review and test.

## Level 3: Live Limited Pilot

Examples: sending to 25 referral partners, routing 10 leads, posting one approved social item.

Approval: required.

Eval: full eval, simulation, monitoring, rollback.

## Level 4: Scaled External Execution

Examples: full campaign, live CRM automation, auto-posting content, production deployment.

Approval: required.

Eval: full zero-trust chain, pilot evidence, stop conditions.

## Level 5: Material Strategic / High Risk

Examples: pricing, offer, target customer, service line, HR changes, legal/finance/tax-sensitive decisions, irreversible changes.

Approval: owner/partner and human expert review when relevant.

Eval: maximum review.
